Vet Care Disclaimer

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Whelpora is not a veterinarian.

Whelpora is a record-keeping and workflow tool built for dog breeders. It does not provide veterinary advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it does not replace the professional judgement of a licensed veterinary surgeon.

What Whelpora is

Whelpora helps you record and organise information about your dogs, litters, whelps, health events, and paperwork. Features such as whelping timers, weight charts, contraction logs, dystocia alerts, oxytocin interlocks, Apgar scoring, vitals checks, and reminders are provided to help you keep clear records and prompt you to act, not to make clinical decisions on your behalf.

What Whelpora is not

  • Whelpora is not a substitute for a physical examination by a veterinarian.
  • Whelpora does not diagnose illness or injury in your dogs or puppies.
  • Whelpora does not prescribe, recommend, or authorise medication, dosages, or procedures.
  • Whelpora does not provide emergency medical services and cannot summon help on your behalf.

You are responsible for the care of your dogs

By using Whelpora you confirm that you are the person responsible for the welfare of the dogs, dams, and puppies whose records you enter, and that you will seek qualified veterinary care whenever it is required. If your dam or a puppy shows signs of distress, illness, or complications during whelping or at any other time, contact a veterinarian immediately. Do not rely on Whelpora timers, thresholds, or alerts as a reason to delay veterinary attention.

Clinical thresholds are guides, not rules

Any thresholds shown in Whelpora (for example, elapsed time between births, dam temperature ranges, oxytocin timing warnings, or Apgar scoring bands) are drawn from widely published breeder and veterinary reference material. They are provided as prompts to help you notice when something may be off, not as clinical rules. Your veterinarian's advice for your specific dog always takes precedence.

Emergencies

If you are facing an urgent situation, close the web, iOS, or Android app and telephone your regular veterinary practice or your nearest out-of-hours emergency vet. Whelpora cannot contact a vet, ambulance, or emergency service on your behalf.
