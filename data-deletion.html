Data Deletion

Last updated: 25 October 2026

You are in control of your data. You can permanently delete your Whelpora account and the personal data associated with it at any time, directly from inside the app.

Quick path

Sign in → Settings → Profile & Account → Delete Account → follow the prompts. You will be asked to re-enter your password and confirm.

How to delete your account

  1. Sign in to Whelpora on the web, iOS, or Android app.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Profile & Account, then Delete My Account. You can download a copy of your data first.
  4. Enter your account password to confirm it is really you.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

The 14-day grace period

When you request account deletion, your account is closed and scheduled for permanent deletion 14 days later. During those 14 days:

  • You are signed out of every device and your account is suspended.
  • Any active paid subscription is set to cancel at the end of the current billing period.
  • You can restore your account at any time using the link in the confirmation email. Restoring reactivates your subscription and gives you full access again.

After 14 days the deletion runs automatically and cannot be reversed.

What is deleted

Permanent deletion removes:

  • Your login account and profile.
  • Your kennel, dogs, litters, puppies, whelping records, health records, medications, weights, photos, tasks, and reminders.
  • Customers, contacts, unsigned contracts, invoices, receipts, and other paperwork you created.
  • Push-notification tokens, cookie preferences, and app settings.
  • Your database records from active storage. They fall out of our daily backup rotation within approximately 7 days.

What may be retained

A small amount of information may be retained after deletion where the law requires it or where it is needed to protect Whelpora or third parties. This can include:

  • Signed contracts. Contracts that were signed electronically through Whelpora, or a signed copy you uploaded, are kept for 7 years after deletion with the signed PDF and the signing record. This lets each buyer still download their copy and lets the signature be proven if there is a dispute. Nobody can open them in Whelpora, and they are deleted at the end of the 7 years.
  • A record of which version of our Terms and Privacy Policy you accepted, kept for 7 years after deletion.
  • Basic records of your Whelpora subscription payments (date, amount, tax) needed to comply with accounting and tax law.
  • Records of security incidents, fraud, chargebacks, or abuse of the service.
  • Copies of any communications you sent to our support team.

These records are minimised, kept only for as long as the law or a legitimate business reason requires, and are never used for marketing.

Kennels with team members

If you own a kennel and other people are members of it, you need to remove them (or transfer ownership) before you can delete your account.

Requesting deletion without signing in

If you can no longer sign in, email support@whelpora.com from the address associated with your account and ask for deletion. We will verify your identity and complete the request within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, the Australian Privacy Act, PIPEDA, or state privacy laws in the US) to access, correct, or receive a copy of your data. Contact support@whelpora.com to exercise any of these rights. Our full Privacy Policy explains this in more detail.
