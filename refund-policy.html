Refund Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Whelpora is a subscription service for dog breeders. You choose either a monthly or an annual plan and you are billed in advance for the plan period. This page explains what happens if you cancel and what refunds we offer.

Short version:

Cancel any time and keep access until the end of your paid period. Apart from confirmed billing errors, and your rights under consumer law, we do not issue refunds for part of a billing period.

Cancelling your subscription

You can cancel your subscription at any time from Settings → Your Plan inside the app. When you cancel:

  • Your paid access continues until the end of the current billing period you have already paid for.
  • You are not charged again after that period.
  • Your data remains available in your account until you also request account deletion.

Refunds

Because Whelpora is a digital service that you can use from the moment it is activated, we do not provide refunds or partial credits for time remaining in the current billing period once it has started, except for confirmed billing errors (such as duplicate charges or charges that occurred after you cancelled) and where consumer law gives you a right to one.

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law or other consumer laws that cannot be excluded. For example, if the service has a major failure, you may be entitled to a refund.

Duplicate or accidental charges

If you were charged twice for the same period, charged after cancelling, or believe there has been a billing error, contact support@whelpora.com within 30 days of the charge. We will investigate and refund confirmed errors in full.

EU and UK statutory withdrawal rights

If you are a consumer in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have a statutory right to withdraw from your subscription within 14 days of first subscribing, without giving a reason. To withdraw, email support@whelpora.com within those 14 days and we will refund that first payment in full. This applies in addition to the rest of this policy.

App Store and Google Play purchases

If you subscribed to Whelpora through Apple's App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google handles billing on our behalf and their own refund rules apply. You will need to request refunds through them directly:

  • Apple App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory

Founding member pricing

The founding member rate is a discount, not a refundable credit. If you cancel, your founding rate is reserved for 12 months; resubscribe within that time and it is reinstated. After 12 months, or if you delete your account, it ends. Clause 6A of our Terms sets out the full rules.

Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is wrong, please contact us first at support@whelpora.com. We will almost always be able to resolve it quickly. Filing a chargeback with your bank without contacting us may result in your account being suspended while the dispute is investigated.

Contact

Questions about billing or refunds: support@whelpora.com.
